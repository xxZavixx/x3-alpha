Last updated: June 2026

We respect your privacy. This policy explains how we collect, use, and protect the information you provide to X3 Alpha.

Security: We use reasonable administrative and technical measures to protect your data, but no system is perfectly secure.

Questions: Contact us at alpha@x3digitalcapital.com for privacy requests.
