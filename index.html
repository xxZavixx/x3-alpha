Frequently Asked Questions
Everything you need to know about X3 Alpha.
What is X3 Alpha?
X3 Alpha is an AI-powered trading journal. You log the trades you've taken, and it reviews them like a performance coach — calculating your stats and pointing out your strengths, recurring mistakes, and behavioral patterns so you can improve.
How does it work?
Add each trade (symbol, side, entry, exit, quantity, date). X3 Alpha instantly calculates your net P/L, win rate, profit factor, and average win/loss. Click Analyze with AI and X3 Alpha analyzes your trades and returns a written breakdown with an action plan for your next sessions.
What do I get with Pro?
The free plan includes a limited number of AI analyses per day. Pro ($29/mo) unlocks unlimited AI analysis, downloadable PDF reports, and priority access to new features. You can cancel anytime.
How do I sign in, and does Pro work on other devices?
Sign-in is passwordless: enter your email and we send a one-tap link. Your Pro membership is tied to your email, not your device — so once you're Pro, just sign in on any phone or computer and it's there. Important: pay with the same email you sign in with, so your purchase links to your account automatically.
Is this financial advice?
No. X3 Alpha reviews trades you've already made and is for informational and educational use only. It never tells you what to buy or sell and makes no predictions. Trading carries substantial risk, and all decisions are your own.
What happens to my data?
Your trades are sent to the AI only to generate your analysis. They aren't displayed publicly. You can read our Privacy Policy below.
How do I cancel Pro?
Pro is billed monthly through Stripe and can be cancelled at any time from the billing portal link in your Stripe receipt email. You keep access until the end of the current billing period.
